अध्याय १ — The Artist

“Folklore, rituals and the rhythms of nature — a world shaped by collective memory.”

Pradip Kumar Jha's woodcuts and emboss prints are rooted in the tribal culture and traditions of his childhood in Sanhouli village, Bihar. The tactile quality of his prints — carved into wood, inked, and pressed by hand — gives them a powerful material presence.

A student of the renowned printmaker Shyam Sharma, he acknowledges his mentor's influence, yet his work stays firmly grounded in his own lived experience — simplified forms, rhythmic patterns, and symbols that remember.
